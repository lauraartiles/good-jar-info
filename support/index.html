Help & Support

Answers to common Good Jar questions and a simple way to get in touch.

Where is my data stored?

Your habits, completions, rewards, and token activity are currently stored on your device. Good Jar does not require an account or use its own cloud service.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Good Jar also deletes its locally stored data. Export and cloud sync are not available yet, so keep the app installed if you want to retain your history.

Do tokens have monetary value?

No. Good Jar tokens are virtual points for personal motivation. They cannot be purchased, transferred, exchanged for cash, or used outside the app.

Can I correct an earlier completion?

Yes. From Today, use the date controls to move to a previous day, then update the completion. You can also log an unscheduled habit when you complete it an extra time.

Contact support

Email support@good-jar.com and include your app version, iOS version, and a description of what happened.
