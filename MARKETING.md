# Good Jar launch kit

## Launch post

Good Jar is officially available on the App Store ✨

I built Good Jar to make everyday habits feel a little more joyful. Complete the habits that matter to you, earn motivational tokens, and spend them on rewards you choose.

You can create flexible weekly goals, revisit previous days, follow your streaks, see weekly recaps, and build a wishlist that makes spending feel more intentional.

Tokens are just for motivation, never real money, and your data stays on your device.
